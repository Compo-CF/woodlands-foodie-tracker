The Woodlands Foodie Tracker

A little paper companion for every spot you've tried, every tier you'd put them in, and the ones still on your must-try list — sent to anyone who joins The Woodlands Dispatch, a free monthly-ish newsletter about Woodlands restaurants, trails, and lakes.

What's inside

  • 1Visited log — a running list with cuisine, area, and notes columns, so you remember why you loved it (or didn't).
  • 2Tier list sheet — classic S/A/B/C/F with space to write five restaurants per tier. Fill in by hand on the couch.
  • 3Must-try column — the ones you keep hearing about but haven't gotten to yet. Cross off as you go.
  • 4Monthly favorites — one page for every month of the year. Great for a “best of” roundup at the end of the year.
